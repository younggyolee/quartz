# Tech pouch
## Cable
- Anker 1.8m usb-c to usb-c 100w cable
- Caldigit Thunderbolt 3 cable
	- This is the ultra beast for everything. It can transmit data for most of the cases (like portable monitor, fast data transfer, wired hotspot to iphone, etc...). The only downside is its length (0.5~0.8m).
	- This is a passive thunderbolt cable which works the best, and I believe its length limit is around 1m due to technical limits. (I haven't tried longer thunderbolt 5 cable like 2 meter ones. Or actually I have 2m-ish cable from my 4k LG Ultrafine monitor. I may just try that for my next trip)
- USAMS 6 in 1 cable
- Hagibis usb c to c short cable (usb 4)

## Audio
- Bose QC35 II Gaming edition
- Sennheiser EPOS 860T
	- USB-C, super simple (no worries about battery, bluetooth connection, etc.) - This is important, because quite often being able to use quickly important when joining a video call (e.g. someone rings you for a video call so your phone/computer is ringing, or you are running late for a call...)
	- Noise cancelling microphone - super quality. People can't here background noise even when I am at a noisy coffee shop. The only downside is that it does catch wind noise (if a fan is directed towards me, it creates unpleasant noises)
	- Noise cancellation for listening - It's quite great at cancelling noises. Only downside is that it's on-ear not over-ear, so there can be more noises coming in by its nature and it can be more uncomfortable for long use.
	- Listening sound quality is actually quite good, up to sennheiser quality. Music on this thing is pleasant to hear.
- Apple wired earpods (usb-c)
	- for backup

## Jacket
- Patagonia torrentshell (for all weathers)
	- Windbreaker with breathability
	- Even in South East Asia where the weather's hot all year round, indoors tend to be quite cold. Thus it's always in my bag all year round.
- Patagonia Down Sweater jacket
	- (for autumn - mild winter weather, of 0-15 degrees celcius)
	- Paired with the Patagonia Torrentshell jacket above, this can cover me for some winter weather (also with Uniqlo heattech - maybe can keep me warm even at 0 degrees)
- Patagonia Frozen Range (or Stormshadow) jacket
	- (for winter weather, lower than than 5 degrees celcius)
	- Super warm even in very cold weather of lower than -10 degrees even without heat tech
	- Quite bulky - though it's compressible, it will still take a quarter of my carry on suitcase.
